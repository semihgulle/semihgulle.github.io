I am a rheumatologist in İzmir working on spondyloarthritis — how it behaves in real-world registries, and what happens in the tissue before it becomes visible in the clinic.

Clinically I run a general academic rheumatology practice with a focus on axial spondyloarthritis, psoriatic arthritis and systemic sclerosis, including musculoskeletal ultrasound of peripheral joints and entheses. My research runs on two tracks: outcomes from the TURKBIO registry and the EuroSpA collaboration, and experimental work on fibrosis and enthesitis in animal models, carried out through the Department of Laboratory Animal Science.

Research

Axial spondyloarthritis and psoriatic arthritis

Treatment response, drug survival, switching and swapping of biologics in real-life cohorts, using TURKBIO and EuroSpA data.

Ultrasound of the enthesis

Ongoing PhD work on high-resolution ultrasound in an experimental spondyloarthritis model, validated against histopathology.

Fibrosis in systemic sclerosis

Skin and lung fibrosis in the bleomycin mouse model, including the effect of JAK1/2 inhibition.

Autoinflammatory diseases

Clinical phenotypes and long-term outcomes of monogenic and undifferentiated autoinflammatory syndromes.
